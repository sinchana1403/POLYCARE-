How It Works

Three Layers of Intelligence

POLYCARE's multimodal pipeline combines three complementary AI subsystems for a comprehensive, reliable PCOS assessment.

— 01 📋

Structured Clinical Data

Patient vitals, hormone levels (LH, FSH, AMH, TSH), BMI, menstrual history, and lifestyle factors are processed by a Random Forest + XGBoost ensemble trained on 2,000+ clinical records.

Random Forest XGBoost 30+ features
— 02 🖼️

Ultrasound Imaging AI

Pelvic/transvaginal ultrasound images are processed by EfficientNetB0 (transfer learning), detecting ovarian morphology patterns characteristic of PCOS — including follicle distribution and ovarian volume.

EfficientNetB0 224×224 input Grad-CAM
— 03 🔗

Late Fusion Decision

Probability outputs from both models are combined using a weighted late-fusion strategy, producing a final confidence score with SHAP-powered explainability for clinical transparency.

Late Fusion SHAP Values Risk Levels
