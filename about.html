The Problem

What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is the most common endocrine disorder in women of reproductive age, affecting approximately 1 in 10 women globally. It is characterised by hormonal imbalances, irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgens, and polycystic ovarian morphology.

Despite its prevalence, PCOS is frequently misdiagnosed or diagnosed late due to variability in its clinical presentation and the subjectivity of ultrasound interpretation. Delayed treatment is associated with infertility, metabolic syndrome, and increased cardiovascular risk.

Emerging Medical Perspective

PCOS vs PMOS: Understanding the
Evolving Perspective

Modern research is reframing polycystic ovary syndrome as a broader metabolic condition — and POLYCARE's model already captures this shift.

Traditional View
🔬

PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome

Classically defined by the Rotterdam Criteria: two of three features — irregular cycles, elevated androgens, and polycystic ovarian morphology on ultrasound. Focus is primarily reproductive and hormonal.

Irregular Cycles Elevated Androgens Ovarian Morphology
vs
Emerging View
🧬

PMOS

Polycystic Metabolic-Ovarian Syndrome

A proposed reframing that places metabolic dysfunction — insulin resistance, obesity, glucose irregularity — at the centre of the condition, recognising it as a systemic metabolic disorder alongside its reproductive features.

Why This Perspective Matters

Studies show that up to 70–80% of PCOS patients have underlying insulin resistance — yet traditional diagnosis often overlooks metabolic markers entirely. The PMOS lens ensures women receive holistic treatment that addresses cardiovascular and diabetic risk, not just fertility outcomes.

Holistic Diagnosis Early Intervention

Research Foundation

Random Forest & Gradient Boosting for PCOS Classification

Demonstrated that ensemble tree-based models outperform single classifiers on structured clinical PCOS data, achieving precision above 95%.

CNN-Based Ultrasound Analysis for Ovarian Morphology
Patel et al. (2022) · IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging

Developed a ResNet-based CNN capable of distinguishing polycystic ovaries from normal morphology in pelvic ultrasound images with >90% accuracy.

CNN / Deep Learning Ultrasound Imaging
Dual-Input Multimodal Learning for PCOS: Late Fusion Approach
Singh et al. (2023) · Computers in Biology and Medicine

Proposed a dual-input system combining structured clinical data and ultrasound images via late fusion, outperforming unimodal approaches by 4–6% in F1-score.
